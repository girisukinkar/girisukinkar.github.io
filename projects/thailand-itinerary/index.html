🇹🇭 Thailand Travel Guide

Travel Rules for Indian Tourists (Updated September 2026)

Effective from 15 September 2026

1. Visa & Entry Rules for Indians

✅ Visa-Free Entry

  • Indian ordinary passport holders can enter visa-free for tourism
  • Maximum stay: 30 days (reduced from the previous 60-day exemption)
  • Completely free — no visa application and no Visa on Arrival fee
  • Process: Simply present passport at immigration counter

🔄 Extension

  • You can extend the 30-day stay once by another 30 days
  • Extension location: Any Thai Immigration office
  • Extension fee: 1,900 THB

⚠️ Important Notes

  • Entries on or before 14 September 2026 still receive the old 60-day stamp
  • Land-border entries (Cambodia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar) are limited to maximum 2 visa-exempt entries per calendar year

2. Documents Required Before Arrival

Requirement Details
Passport Valid for at least 6 months from arrival date + blank pages
TDAC Mandatory – Thailand Digital Arrival Card (free online form)
Return / Onward Ticket Confirmed ticket showing departure within allowed stay
Accommodation Proof Hotel booking or address of stay
Proof of Funds Recommended 20,000 THB per person (cash preferred)

🌐 How to Complete TDAC (Mandatory)

  • Official website: https://tdac.immigration.go.th
  • Submit within 72 hours (3 days) before arrival
  • Required info: Passport details, flight number, arrival date, first accommodation address
  • You will receive: QR code / confirmation email (keep it ready on phone or printed)

⛔ Warning

Only use the official site (https://tdac.immigration.go.th). Third-party sites that charge money are scams.

3. After Arrival – Rules to Follow

📍 Immigration & Stay

  • You will get a 30-day entry stamp (from 15 Sept 2026)
  • Do NOT overstay
    • Fine: 500 THB per day (maximum 20,000 THB)
    • Longer overstays can result in re-entry bans (1–10 years)

🏨 TM30 Address Reporting

  • Hotels and registered guesthouses file this automatically
  • If staying in private home / Airbnb / with friends or family → the host/landlord must report within 24 hours
  • Keep a copy of the TM30 receipt if you plan to extend your stay

4. Customs Rules (What You Can Bring)

Item Allowed Limit Notes
Alcohol Maximum 1 litre Excess may be confiscated
Cigarettes Maximum 200 cigarettes or 250g tobacco Per person
E-cigarettes / Vapes Completely banned Import, possession or use is illegal. Heavy fines possible
Foreign Currency Declare if more than 20,000 USD equivalent —
Prescription Medicine Generally allowed in original packaging Controlled drugs may need special permits

🚫 Strictly Prohibited

  • All e-cigarettes, vape pens, e-liquids, and related devices
  • Illegal drugs (very severe penalties, including possible death penalty for trafficking)
  • Recreational cannabis (now medical-only with Thai prescription)

5. Cannabis Rules (2026 Update)

  • Cannabis is medical only
  • You need a valid Thai prescription (PT33) to buy legally
  • Public smoking is illegal (fines up to 25,000 THB)
  • Never take cannabis out of Thailand (treated as trafficking)

6. Cultural & Behaviour Rules

  • Respect the Monarchy: Criticism (including online posts) is a serious criminal offence
  • Temples: Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, speak softly
  • Physical Contact: Do not touch anyone's head (considered sacred)
  • Body Language: Avoid pointing with feet or showing the soles of your feet
  • Dress Code: Dress modestly outside beach areas
